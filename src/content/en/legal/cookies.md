Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. This page explains which cookies and similar technologies the Bistromat website uses.

## Our cookies

**The Bistromat website sets no tracking cookies** and uses no analytics or advertising tools. Fonts and other resources load from our own server, without sending data to third parties.

We remember your cookie choice in your browser's local storage (localStorage, key `bm-consent`) so that we don't ask you on every visit. This record is necessary and is never sent anywhere.

## Third-party content

Some parts of the website show content from other services, which may set their own cookies when that content loads:

| Service | Where it is used | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Google Maps | Home and Contact pages | Showing a map with the restaurant's location |
| Instagram, Facebook | Links in the footer | Links only; cookies are set only when you visit those networks |

**The map loads only once you allow it**, through the cookie notice or the "Show" button on the content itself. Until then, no data is sent to Google.

Those services are responsible for their own cookies. You can find more in their privacy policies, e.g. [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## How to manage cookies

You can change your choice at any time with the **Cookie settings** link in the website footer. You can also view, block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block third-party cookies, the website will still work, but the map or booking form may not appear.

## Changes

If we start using analytics or other cookies, we will update this page and, where required, ask for your consent before setting them.

Send any questions about cookies to [office@bistromat.rs](mailto:office@bistromat.rs). Read more about how we process data in our [Privacy policy](/en/privacy/).
